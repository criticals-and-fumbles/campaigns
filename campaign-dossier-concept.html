Situation Overview

Following the interception of an ore convoy at Cinder Station, recovered data indicates that the stolen biological sample has been transferred to Gates of Dawn, Docking Bay 6, for delivery to a recipient identified only as "Sable."

The transfer was routed through a concealed Arachne‑compatible node that may have been compromised by the same external network responsible for the Baraka Deep‑7 and Cinder Station incidents. Further infiltration of Nomad infrastructure on Dawn is suspected.

Operatives are to establish contact with Sable, determine the purpose of PROJECT MIRRORGLASS, and identify whether the Nomad network was coerced, compromised, or complicit.
