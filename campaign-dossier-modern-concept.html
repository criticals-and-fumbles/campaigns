Background

Three contractors have quit the Kim Seng Road shophouse renovation in as many weeks, each citing the same thing: something moves in the walls after the workers leave. The client wants it handled quietly before the story reaches the press.

The building predates the war and was, according to one elderly neighbour, "never meant to be lived in" — a phrase nobody on the team has managed to get her to explain further.

The team's read: either a very committed prankster, a structural issue nobody wants to admit to, or something the client isn't telling them.
