I.

The Tale So Far

The party descended into the Sundered Vale seeking the last resting place of a fallen titan, where the old stories say its bones still hum with a memory of the world before the Gate closed.

What they found instead was a Frost Giant warband encamped at the vale's mouth, and stranger still, the marks of the Cult of the Fallen Star carved fresh into the stone — a symbol none of the party's scholars could place.

The Guildmaster suspects the two are not unconnected, and has asked the party to determine whether the cult seeks the titan's remains for the same reason the party does — or for something far worse.
